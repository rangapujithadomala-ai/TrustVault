TrustVault Sign in

Share documents securely, with a record of every access.

TrustVault is a document-sharing web application. Owners upload documents and grant read or edit access to specific people. Access is denied by default, and every grant, download and denied attempt is written to an audit log.

Go to sign in

Deny by default

Each request is checked against a live, document-specific permission before anything is shown or downloaded.

Three clear roles

Admins manage accounts and review audit events, Owners upload and share documents, and Viewers open what has been shared with them.

Tamper-evident audit log

Access events are chained with SHA-256 hashes so changes to the history can be detected.

Private storage

Files are kept in private storage and are never exposed through public links.

About this project. TrustVault is an educational prototype built for the Snovasys RISE challenge. It is not affiliated with any bank, payment provider or other company, and it will never ask you for payment details or to download software.
