Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

TrustVault is an educational document-sharing prototype. This page explains what information it handles.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Documents and metadata: files you upload, their titles, categories and sharing permissions.
  • Activity records: sign-ins, document access, downloads and denied attempts, kept in the audit log.

How we use it

Only to operate the service: authenticating you, enforcing who can open a document, sending verification and password-reset emails, and keeping a security audit trail.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show advertising, or ask for payment information. We will never email you asking for your password.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored with our hosting and database providers (Render and Supabase). Documents are shared only with the people the document owner chooses.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you signed in. It is HTTP-only and expires after a period of inactivity.

Your choices
